Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to Snake Business Tycoon. We respect your digital privacy and are committed to maintaining a transparent architecture for our deployment platform. This Privacy Policy details how data is parsed and retained on our website.

⚡ Immediate Summary: We do not deploy external servers. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal information. Your game performance data never leaves your physical device.

1. Data Storage Architecture (Local Processing)

This application utilizes browser-based localStorage technology to store game state variables. The exact scopes written to your machine's local repository include:

  • Financial Assets: Virtual balance parameters, active leverage/loan state data, and timestamp values tracking loan repayment schedules.
  • Asset Inventories: Integer strings keeping track of virtual commercial retail properties, manufacturing plants, and residential real estate assets.
  • Reward Timestamps: Local systems tracking constraints on daily game bonus updates to manage gameplay compliance.

2. Zero Third-Party Transmission

Because all game logical calculations execute entirely client-side:

  • No database layers or cloud tracking tools are managed on our backend infrastructure.
  • No cookies, marketing tags, or cross-site tracking profiles are hardcoded or shared with outside advertisement networks.

3. User Data Control and Deletion

You maintain full sovereignty over your local browser data storage. You can instantly clear all stored records from your system using either of these methodologies:

  1. Clicking the "Terminate Corporate Entity" interface function on the primary gaming dashboard.
  2. Clearing your web browser's storage cache via standard technical application settings.

4. Amendments to this Protocol

We reserve the operational authority to update our system rules or legal templates as necessary. Any modifications made will be reflected directly on this page with an updated modification timestamp.

5. Legal Inquiries & Contact

If you have any questions regarding your localized data footprint, please reach out via standard development infrastructure or open an issue on our distribution portal.
