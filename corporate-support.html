Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save my game progress? +

Your progress is automatically saved to your web browser's local cache storage continuously. No manual saving or accounts are required.

I lost my tycoon assets and balance. Can I get them back? +

Because your data is stored locally on your physical machine, clearing your browser history, clear cookies, or running system cleaning software will delete your local file. We do not store data on server databases and cannot recover lost progress.

The game screen is black or won't load. What do I do? +

Ensure your internet browser is updated to the latest version and has JavaScript enabled. Try hard-refreshing your browser application (Ctrl + F5 or Cmd + Shift + R).
